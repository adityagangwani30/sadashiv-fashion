# Sadashiv Fashions --- Website Design Direction & Antigravity Build Brief

**Project:** Sadashiv Fashions website\
**Design direction:** Minimal, editorial, Jaipur / Pink City inspired
boutique and fashion store\
**Website version:** V1.0\
**Audience:** Retail customers and wholesale buyers\
**Primary conversion:** WhatsApp product enquiry

------------------------------------------------------------------------

## 1. Creative direction

Create a polished, understated website for **Sadashiv Fashions**, a
boutique and fashion / textile store in Jaipur.

The site should feel like an independent fashion label with a strong
sense of place: Jaipur's Pink City, its architecture, craft, textiles,
colour and visual warmth. The design should be modern and minimal, with
subtle local character rather than obvious tourist imagery or decorative
clichés.

The overall feeling: - Editorial fashion boutique - Jaipur-rooted and
contemporary - Warm, tactile, refined and welcoming - Product
photography first - Spacious layouts and confident typography - Premium
without looking inaccessible - Designed with restraint and a clear
visual hierarchy

**Do not make it look like an AI-generated website, generic Shopify
theme, SaaS landing page or over-decorated wedding invitation.**

The finished website should look like a real designer / creative studio
made thoughtful decisions for a real Jaipur fashion business.

------------------------------------------------------------------------

## 2. Visual principles

### 2.1 Restraint over decoration

Use a small set of colours, a clear type system and consistent spacing.
Do not add decorative elements simply to fill empty space.

### 2.2 Real products are the design

The actual Sadashiv Fashions product photographs should carry the visual
identity. Build layouts around the shape, colour and texture of the real
garments and fabrics.

### 2.3 Jaipur as an influence, not a theme park

Use subtle references to: - Jaipur's warm pink sandstone / rose plaster
tones - Havelis, jharokhas and arched architectural proportions -
Hand-block printing, textile pattern and craft - Sunlit courtyards,
old-city textures and warm natural light

Do not use random palace stock photos, tourist collages, elephant
motifs, excessive paisley, ornamental borders or stereotypical
Rajasthani graphics.

### 2.4 Editorial composition

Use asymmetry where appropriate, generous negative space, strong
photography crops, restrained captions and deliberate section pacing.
Avoid making every section a centred card grid.

------------------------------------------------------------------------

## 4. Typography

Use a refined, readable typography system. Suggested pairings: -
**Headings:** Cormorant Garamond, DM Serif Display or another restrained
editorial serif. - **Body / UI:** Inter, Manrope or a similarly clean
sans serif. - **Small labels:** the body sans serif in uppercase with
modest letter spacing.

Use no more than two font families plus an optional mono face for tiny
metadata. Keep body copy readable and avoid excessively thin weights.

Typography should feel like a fashion editorial, not a luxury-brand
parody: - Large but controlled hero heading - Strong contrast between
display heading and body copy - Comfortable line length - Short
paragraphs - Small uppercase category labels - No giant all-caps text
everywhere

## 5. Photography and image treatment

### 5.1 Source imagery

Use genuine Sadashiv Fashions assets: - Existing logo - Actual suits -
Actual kurtis - Actual fabrics - Actual dupattas - Store exterior and
interior - Portraits of Suresh Gangwani and Kashish Gangwani

The owner has said these assets are available online / with the
business. Use owner-provided files or images from official business
channels where use is permitted. Do not assume a random search image is
a real product.

### 5.2 Image rules

-   Product imagery must be accurate and unaltered in ways that
    misrepresent the product.
-   Prefer natural light and true-to-life colours.
-   Preserve textile texture and embroidery detail.
-   Use a mix of portrait, landscape and close-up crops.
-   Use consistent image ratios within a component, but vary composition
    across the page.
-   Avoid heavy filters, fake film grain, excessive blur,
    plastic-looking AI imagery and overly smooth skin.
-   Do not generate synthetic product photography or fake owner
    portraits.
-   Never use generated or unrelated people to represent the owners.

### 5.3 Image layout

-   Hero: one strong editorial photograph, preferably a real campaign /
    store / product image.
-   Category cards: large, clean crops with minimal labels.
-   Fabrics: include close-up detail images that show weave, print and
    texture.
-   Product cards: consistent crop ratio, neutral backgrounds where
    possible.
-   About page: genuine owner portraits, ideally photographed in the
    store or against a simple natural background.
-   Store section: real interior / exterior images to establish local
    credibility.

### 5.4 Asset handling

Download approved images into the project. Optimize with Next.js Image
or an equivalent pipeline. Use descriptive filenames and meaningful alt
text. Do not hotlink Instagram or directory image URLs as the production
image source.

## 6. Page-by-page design direction

### 6.1 Home page

**Header** - Existing logo on the left. - Minimal navigation: Home,
Collections, Our Story, Gallery, Visit Us. - One restrained WhatsApp
enquiry action. - Keep the header compact and elegant. - Mobile
navigation should be simple and accessible.

**Hero** - Large editorial image with a deliberate crop. - Small eyebrow
label such as `JAIPUR · ETHNIC WEAR & TEXTILES`. - One short headline.
Possible direction: `A little Jaipur in every detail.` or
`Tradition, thoughtfully styled.` - One short supporting sentence
describing the real collections. - Primary action:
`Explore Collections`. - Secondary action: `Enquire on WhatsApp`. -
Avoid generic claims like "Where elegance meets tradition" unless the
owners genuinely want that language.

**Brand introduction** - A short, factual introduction to Sadashiv
Fashions. - Avoid invented heritage, artisan, craftsmanship or
family-business claims.

**Collections** - Four categories: Suits, Kurtis, Fabrics, Dupattas. -
Use real product images. - Do not make every tile a rounded card with a
shadow. - Use image-led editorial tiles with clear labels.

**Selected products / new arrivals** - Show only real products with
owner-confirmed names. - Use restrained product cards. - No fake prices,
ratings, sale badges or stock labels. - CTA: `Enquire`.

**Retail and wholesale** - A concise section that clearly welcomes
individual and wholesale enquiries. - Separate WhatsApp message
templates for retail and wholesale if helpful.

**About preview** - A quiet, image-led introduction to the people behind
the store. - Link to the About page.

**Visit the store** - Address and location details only after owner
verification. - Use an actual store image. - Include a directions link
and WhatsApp CTA.

**Footer** - Logo, short business description, page links, Instagram,
WhatsApp, address and verified timings. - Keep it simple and useful.

### 6.2 Collections page

-   Page introduction with a strong image or understated heading.
-   Four clear category selectors.
-   Product listing with consistent image crops and clean spacing.
-   Optional category filter if catalogue size justifies it.
-   Each product card includes product name, category and
    `Enquire on WhatsApp`.
-   Do not add filters that do not map to real product data.
-   Avoid fake inventory, prices and product ratings.

### 6.3 Product detail view / page

-   Large image gallery with thumbnails or a clean stacked layout.
-   Product name and category.
-   Only confirmed material / size / colour / length details.
-   A concise description, not generic AI-written marketing copy.
-   WhatsApp enquiry CTA with product name and page URL prefilled.
-   Related products only when actual related products exist.

### 6.4 About page --- owners are a key feature

This page should feel personal, genuine and editorial. It is not a
corporate "Our Team" page.

**Page heading direction:** `The people behind Sadashiv Fashions`

**Intro copy direction:**
`Sadashiv Fashions is a Jaipur-based fashion and textile store, bringing together a considered selection of women's ethnic wear and fabrics. Meet the people behind the store and discover the collections.`

**Owner profiles:** 1. **Suresh Gangwani** 2. **Kashish Gangwani**

Design: - Give each owner a genuine portrait photograph. - Use a large,
natural crop; avoid circular avatar portraits. - Place the name clearly
beside or below the portrait. - Use a short owner-approved role / title
and bio. - Consider a two-column editorial layout on desktop and a
simple stacked layout on mobile. - Include one candid / store-context
image if available. - Keep the layout warm and human, with generous
whitespace.

Important content rules: - Do not invent family relationships, founder
status, job titles, responsibilities or personal biographies. - Ask the
owners to approve the photographs and text. - Do not use AI-generated
portraits or unrelated stock people.

### 6.5 Gallery page

-   A considered visual journal of store, collections and textile
    details.
-   Mix wide store photos with close-up product images.
-   Use a masonry-like or editorial grid only if it remains orderly and
    responsive.
-   Link to Instagram for more frequent updates.
-   Do not embed an unreliable third-party Instagram widget if it harms
    performance or requires fragile access.

### 6.6 Contact / Visit Us

-   Store address
-   Confirmed hours
-   WhatsApp number
-   Instagram link
-   Google Maps directions
-   Brief note that local delivery is available in Jaipur and nearby
    areas; exact delivery availability and charges are confirmed through
    WhatsApp.

Use a clean contact layout, not a large form with unnecessary fields.

## 7. Components and interaction details

### Product card

-   Image
-   Product name
-   Category / concise detail
-   Text link or understated button: `Enquire on WhatsApp`
-   Entire card should not unexpectedly navigate if the only intended
    action is WhatsApp; make the interaction clear.

### WhatsApp action

-   Use `https://wa.me/916376648869`.
-   Prefill a contextual message.
-   Use a small WhatsApp icon only where it improves recognition.
-   Do not cover the screen with an intrusive floating widget.
-   A discreet fixed WhatsApp action on mobile is acceptable if it does
    not obscure content.

### Navigation

-   Clear active states.
-   Smooth but restrained transitions.
-   Keyboard accessible.
-   No over-engineered mega menu.

### Motion

-   Subtle fades / small translate transitions.
-   Gentle image reveal if performance remains good.
-   Respect `prefers-reduced-motion`.
-   No constant floating, parallax overload, cursor trails, scroll
    hijacking or bouncing elements.

------------------------------------------------------------------------

## 8. Layout, spacing and responsive behaviour

-   Design mobile-first, then refine tablet and desktop.
-   Use a consistent spacing scale.
-   Use a readable maximum content width.
-   Keep text columns narrow enough for comfortable reading.
-   Use generous vertical rhythm, but avoid enormous empty sections.
-   Use a consistent grid and alignment system.
-   Avoid a wall of cards.
-   Ensure buttons, navigation and WhatsApp links are comfortable to
    tap.
-   Product imagery should remain the focal point on mobile.
-   Do not shrink desktop layouts mechanically; redesign compositions
    for narrow screens.

------------------------------------------------------------------------

## 9. Technical direction

Recommended stack: - Next.js with App Router - TypeScript - Tailwind
CSS - Next.js Image for image optimization - Static / local product data
(JSON or TypeScript) - Vercel deployment

V1.0 does not need a database, authentication, admin panel, checkout or
payment gateway.

Suggested project organization: - `app/` for routes and layouts -
`components/` for reusable UI - `data/products.ts` or
`data/products.json` for catalogue entries - `public/images/` for
approved optimized assets - `public/brand/` for logo and identity files

Create reusable components for: - Header / mobile navigation - Footer -
Hero - Collection tile - Product card - Product gallery - WhatsApp
enquiry link - Owner profile - Store / contact block

SEO: - Unique title and description for each page. - Open Graph
metadata. - Sitemap and robots file. - LocalBusiness / ClothingStore
structured data with verified facts only. - Descriptive image alt
text. - Semantic HTML and accessible heading order.

Performance: - Optimize image sizes and formats. - Lazy-load
below-the-fold imagery. - Avoid unnecessary animation libraries. - Avoid
excessive client-side JavaScript. - Keep Core Web Vitals in mind.

## 10. Anti-AI / anti-template quality checklist

Before considering the design complete, inspect it against this list:

-   [ ] Does the site look like a real boutique brand rather than a
    generic AI landing page?
-   [ ] Are the images genuine Sadashiv Fashions assets?
-   [ ] Does the design use a distinctive but restrained Jaipur visual
    language?
-   [ ] Is there enough whitespace without wasting screen space?
-   [ ] Are there varied layouts rather than identical repeated cards?
-   [ ] Are headings concise and specific?
-   [ ] Has generic marketing filler been removed?
-   [ ] Are gradients, glows, glassmorphism and excessive shadows
    avoided?
-   [ ] Are rounded cards used only when they improve the interface?
-   [ ] Is typography consistent and editorial?
-   [ ] Are all product details and business claims factual?
-   [ ] Does the About page use real approved portraits of both owners?
-   [ ] Are mobile layouts intentionally designed?
-   [ ] Are WhatsApp links working and prefilled correctly?
-   [ ] Is every page complete, with no placeholder copy or broken
    image?
-   [ ] Are accessibility, contrast, keyboard navigation and reduced
    motion considered?

### Avoid these common AI-generated design patterns

-   Purple / blue gradients on a fashion website.
-   Every section inside a rounded rectangle.
-   Repeated three-card feature grids.
-   Oversized pill buttons everywhere.
-   Fake statistics such as "10k+ happy customers".
-   Made-up testimonials and star ratings.
-   Generic headings repeated across pages.
-   Stock photography that does not represent the actual store.
-   Decorative blobs, random icons and excessive floating elements.
-   Unnecessary animations and scroll effects.
-   Generic AI copy such as "Discover the perfect blend of tradition and
    modernity".

## 11. Content and asset rules for Antigravity

Treat the companion business brief as the source of truth for facts and
website copy.

Priority order for content: 1. Owner-confirmed facts and assets. 2.
Official Sadashiv Fashions Instagram / owner-provided images. 3.
Verified public business listings for factual cross-checking. 4.
Carefully written neutral copy where no specific fact is available.

Never: - Invent product names, prices, sizes or availability. - Invent
business history, owner biographies or relationships. - Use a
third-party listing's phone number instead of the confirmed WhatsApp
number. - Claim pan-India shipping; current delivery scope is Jaipur and
nearby areas. - Build a checkout or admin system; neither is in V1.0
scope. - Use AI-generated product images or owner portraits.

If a required asset or fact is missing, create a clear TODO in the
project rather than fabricating it.

## 12. Final implementation brief for Antigravity

Fashions using the two project documents:

-   `01-business-content-brief.md` --- business facts, pages, products,
    contact details and content rules.

    technical guidance and quality standards.

First inspect the available logo and genuine product / store
photographs. Build the design around those real assets. Do not begin by
generating generic placeholder images.

Create a distinctive, minimal, editorial boutique experience inspired by
Jaipur and the Pink City. Use warm ivory, subtle rose / terracotta and
deep maroon-brown only after checking the existing logo. Use refined
typography, generous whitespace, varied editorial compositions and
high-quality responsive layouts.

The site should include: - Home - Collections (Suits, Kurtis, Fabrics,
Dupattas) - Product enquiry experiences - About page featuring Suresh
Gangwani and Kashish Gangwani with real owner-approved photographs and
bios - Gallery - Contact / Visit Us

The main conversion is WhatsApp enquiry to `+91 63766 48869`. Support
both retail and wholesale enquiries with appropriate prefilled messages.
Local delivery is limited to Jaipur and nearby areas.

Use Next.js, TypeScript and Tailwind CSS. Use static product data and
optimized local assets. Do not build an admin panel, database, shopping
cart, checkout or payment gateway.

Work through the whole site, not just a homepage mockup. Ensure all
navigation and links work, all pages are populated with factual content,
the mobile experience is polished, and the final result passes the
anti-AI / anti-template checklist above.
