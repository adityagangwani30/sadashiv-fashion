## 1. Business overview

Sadashiv Fashions is a Jaipur-based fashion and textile business serving
both retail customers and wholesale buyers. Its website should present
the business as a boutique and fashion store with a curated range of
women's ethnic wear and fabrics.

The website is a digital catalogue and enquiry channel, not a full
online store. Visitors should be able to discover collections, view
product photography and contact the business on WhatsApp to ask about
prices, availability, sizes, quantities and local delivery.

### Positioning to communicate

-   Jaipur-based boutique and fashion / textile store.
-   Women's ethnic wear and fabric collections.
-   Retail and wholesale enquiries are welcome.
-   Customers can browse online and enquire directly.
-   Store visits and local delivery in Jaipur and nearby areas.

Do not invent a founding story, heritage claim, artisan network,
manufacturing capability, years of experience, awards, guarantees or
product-quality claims. Use only details confirmed by the owners.

## 7. Retail and wholesale journeys

The website serves two audiences. Make the distinction clear without
making the interface complicated.

### Retail customer

1.  Opens a category.
2.  Views a product.
3.  Clicks "Enquire on WhatsApp".
4.  Asks about price, availability, size / quantity and delivery.
5.  Team confirms details.

### Wholesale buyer

1.  Opens the website.
2.  Sees a visible "Wholesale enquiries welcome" note.
3.  Views relevant fabric / product categories.
4.  Clicks "Wholesale enquiry".
5.  Sends approximate quantity, product interest and business details
    over WhatsApp.

Suggested wholesale prefilled message:

> Hello Sadashiv Fashions,
>
> I am enquiring about a wholesale purchase. Products of interest:
> \[Product / category\] Approximate quantity: \[Quantity\] City:
> \[City\]
>
> Please share your catalogue, pricing and terms.

Do not promise minimum order quantities, wholesale rates, shipping
outside Jaipur or dispatch timelines until confirmed.

## 11. V1.0 scope boundaries

**Include** - Responsive informational website - Home, Collections,
About, Gallery and Contact pages - Product cards / detail views -
WhatsApp enquiry links with prefilled messages - Instagram link - Google
Maps directions - Basic technical and local SEO - Optimized image assets

**Exclude from V1.0** - E-commerce checkout - Online payments - Cart and
customer accounts - Inventory management - Admin dashboard / CMS -
Automated shipping calculation - Delivery tracking - Product reviews
unless sourced and approved

The site can be built as a static / content-driven Next.js website.
Product data can live in local JSON or TypeScript data files so a
developer can update the catalogue manually.
